FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How do I create my first invoice?

Tap the "+" button on the home screen, fill in your client details, add line items, and tap "Send." Your invoice is created in seconds and can be sent via email, WhatsApp, or PDF download.

Can I use Invo App for free?

Yes! The Free Plan lets you create unlimited invoices with core features. Upgrade to Pro to unlock custom branding, recurring invoices, analytics, and priority support.

How do I set up my business profile?

Go to Settings → Business Profile. Add your business name, logo, address, and tax details. This info appears automatically on every invoice you create.

Can clients pay directly through the invoice?

Yes, on the Pro plan you can connect Stripe or PayPal and your clients will see a "Pay Now" button on their invoice. Payments are tracked automatically in the app.

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within a minute. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account. Type "Delete Account" to confirm. This permanently removes all your data and cannot be undone.

Setup guide

Connect your GoDaddy domain

Follow these steps to point your GoDaddy domain to this support website.

1

Log in to GoDaddy

Go to godaddy.com → sign in → click your name (top right) → My Products → find your domain and click DNS.

2

Update the A record

Find the A record with name @. Click the pencil icon and change the value to your hosting server's IP address (e.g. the IP where this HTML file is hosted). Set TTL to 600.

3

Add a CNAME for www

Add a new CNAME record: name = www, value = yourdomain.com (your root domain). This makes both www. and non-www work.

4

Enable SSL in GoDaddy

Under your domain's settings, look for Security → SSL Certificate. Enable the free SSL (or install your own). This gives your site https:// for trust and security.

5

Wait for propagation

DNS changes take 30 minutes to 48 hours to go live worldwide. You can check progress at dnschecker.org by entering your domain name.

6

Update your Flutter app

In your app's Support tile handler, replace the hardcoded email/WhatsApp constants with your live domain URLs (e.g. https://support.yourdomain.com) using url_launcher to open this page in-app.
