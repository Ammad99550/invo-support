How do I create my first invoice?

Tap the "+" button on the home screen, fill in client details, add line items, and tap "Send." Done in seconds — share via email, WhatsApp, or PDF download.

Can I use Invo App for free?

Yes! The Free Plan gives you unlimited invoices with core features. Upgrade to Pro for custom branding, recurring invoices, payment integrations, and priority support.

How do I set up my business profile?

Go to Settings → Business Profile. Add your logo, address, and tax details. This info appears automatically on every invoice you create.

Can clients pay directly through the invoice?

Yes, on the Pro plan. Connect Stripe or PayPal and clients see a "Pay Now" button. Payments are tracked automatically in the app.

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link in under a minute. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account. Type "Delete Account" to confirm. This permanently removes all data and cannot be undone.

Domain setup

Connect your GoDaddy domain

Point your GoDaddy domain to this support website in 6 steps.

1

Log in to GoDaddy

Go to godaddy.com → sign in → click your name → My Products → find your domain → click DNS.

2

Update the A record

Find the A record with name @. Edit the value to your hosting server's IP address. Set TTL to 600.

3

Add a CNAME for www

Add a new CNAME record: name = www, value = your root domain. This makes both www and non-www versions work.

4

Enable SSL

In GoDaddy → Security → SSL Certificate. Enable the free SSL. This gives your site https:// for security and trust.

5

Wait for propagation

DNS changes take 30 min to 48 hours worldwide. Check progress at dnschecker.org.

6

Update your Flutter app

In your support tile handler, use url_launcher to open https://support.yourdomain.com directly in-app when the user taps Support.
